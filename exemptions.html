Every FOIA (b)(N) redaction marker across the Bioresearch Monitoring (BIMO) release · Pfizer & Moderna COVID-19 vaccines.

Documents Exemptions
Marker counts for the documents that also appear in the PHMPT corpus are reused from the foia.coviddocuments.com exemption scan (they are the same documents, redacted identically). The New documents released only in this FOIA production were OCR-scanned fresh for this report, so their redaction markers appear here for the first time. Non-PDF data files (.xpt/.txt) carry no redaction markers and are counted as zero. OCR is imperfect on dense scans. Only the nine real FOIA exemptions can appear — (b)(1)–(b)(9), with letter subparts only on (b)(7). Counts reflect actual redaction stamps, not citations. The raw (b)(N) text pattern also matches statutory citations (e.g. FD&C Act §564(b)(1), 21 CFR 316.3(b)(13)); every rare-exemption hit was classified by its surrounding context and citations were excluded — which is why (b)(1), (b)(2), (b)(3) and (b)(9), whose occurrences here were all citations, do not appear. (b)(4) and (b)(6) (99.98% of all markers) are counted as redaction stamps.
